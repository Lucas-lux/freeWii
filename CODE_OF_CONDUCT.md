# Code of conduct

freeWii is a small project about a plastic remote and silly mini-games. The bar for behaviour here
is simple: be the kind of person other people want to build things with.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings about a detail nobody wrote down.
- Critique the code, not the person. "This breaks when two remotes disconnect at once" is useful.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time, and that "not now" or "not this project" are valid answers.
- Remember that many contributors are not native English speakers, and that a blunt sentence is
  usually a translation artefact rather than hostility.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or comments about someone's identity, background or private life.
- Sexual language or imagery anywhere in the project.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Persistently derailing issues and pull requests, or re-opening settled decisions to wear people down.
- Pressuring anyone to add copyrighted assets to the repository.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers at **<add-your-contact-address-here>**. Reports are
read only by the maintainers, and you will get an answer. Depending on what happened, the response
ranges from a private word to deleting the content and blocking the account from the repository.

Maintainers who do not follow this document are subject to the same consequences as anyone else.
